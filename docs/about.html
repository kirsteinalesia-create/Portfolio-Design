01 · About me

Curious by nature.
Driven by possibilities.

I'm Alesia Katharina Kirstein, a SAP STAR Student studying Digital Media at DHBW Mannheim, with one clear focus: design. It's not just my field of study. It's my way of thinking, seeing, and creating.

I obsess over the details that most people never notice, from the spacing between elements to the exact shade of a colour and the rhythm of a layout. For me, great design is where aesthetics meet intention. Every visual choice should feel effortless but be deeply considered.

At SAP, I've had the chance to bring this mindset into real products, designing interfaces, brand identities, visual systems, and user experiences from scratch. From Figma prototypes to polished presentations, from user research to pixel perfect handoffs, I've explored every stage of the design process.

When I'm not designing for work, I'm drawing, illustrating, or rethinking something I saw today. Design isn't my career choice. It's my creative home.
