01 · Briefing

Background & Assignment

The "Night of the Graduates" (NotG) is the annual graduation ceremony of DHBW Mannheim, held at the Mannheimer Rosengarten and welcoming several thousand guests. Graduates receive their certificates and celebrate at a festive Grand Opening featuring speeches and performances by the SAP Symphony Orchestra. The evening continues with live music, DJs and walking acts across multiple stages.

The 2026 edition is themed around Artificial Intelligence, reflecting the growing importance of AI in science, industry and society. As part of the five member Print & Digital Media Team, I was responsible for designing and producing key print materials. This included working with the corporate design team's style guide, coordinating professional printing with Heidelberger Druckmaschinen and ensuring a consistent visual identity across all media.

Project Goals

  • Create a cohesive print & digital media portfolio for the NotG 2026
  • Integrate the AI theme consistently across all media
  • Ensure print-readiness with spot colors and professional prepress
  • Accompany the audience along the entire event journey

02 · My Role

Print Design & Production

Within the Print & Digital Media Team, I was responsible for the poster, program booklet and Save the Date postcards. My work covered the entire production process, from initial layout concepts and color calibration to preparing the final prepress files for offset printing at Heidelberger Druckmaschinen.

One of the main challenges was combining spot colors with CMYK while ensuring that the AI themed net pattern and event branding were accurately reproduced in print. Through several rounds of proofs and on site visits to HDM, I gained practical experience in professional print production and quality assurance.

Poster

Developed a new concept and layout for the program booklet poster, building on the existing design language. Optimized color values and prepared final print files.

Program Booklet

Integrated all texts and data provided by university communications, optimized the color systems (CMYK & spot colors), and prepared the booklet for professional printing at HDM.

Save the Date Postcards

Technical color optimization and precise preparation of the final print data for the Save the Date postcards sent to all graduates.

04 · Conclusion

From screen to print — bringing an event to life.

The Night of the Graduates project gave me a deep understanding of professional print production: from spot colors and coordinating with Heidelberger Druckmaschinen to color accuracy and paper choice. Seeing thousands of guests interact with media I helped create was especially rewarding.
