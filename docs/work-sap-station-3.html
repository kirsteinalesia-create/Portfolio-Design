01 · Context

The Research LAB

My team, CX Cross, develops and maintains the Research LAB, an internal SAP platform that supports user researchers in planning, conducting and documenting their studies. During this practical phase, I had the opportunity to write my second project paper (Project Paper 2) on this very tool. I analyzed the existing Research LAB, explored how Artificial Intelligence could improve its workflows and developed a concrete concept for integrating AI into the research process.

02 · Process

From analysis to AI template

I started with expert interviews across SAP to understand how the Research LAB was used, identify pain points and define requirements for an AI-supported solution.

In parallel, I compared different AI research tools, evaluating their usefulness, integration potential and data privacy.

The Research LAB combines all workspaces with an integrated Joule assistant for context-specific questions and a dedicated chat for broader research inquiries.

Based on my findings, I developed an AI-powered synthesis template to help researchers structure and document insights more efficiently. It was integrated into the existing Research LAB and refined through feedback from the user research team.

04 · Takeaways

What I learned at this station

This station deepened my understanding of how AI can support and improve user research processes. I learned to conduct expert interviews, critically evaluate tools and turn research insights into a functional prototype while navigating topics such as data privacy and stakeholder alignment.

Soft Skills

  • Translating findings into actionable design decisions
