01 · My role

My role in the team

During my fourth practical phase, I joined the CS Japan Growth Office in Tokyo. This experience took me beyond my usual working environment and gave me new insights into Presales and Customer Experience. The team works closely with customers to showcase SAP solutions at the Experience Center. My role was to create visual communication materials and customer-facing content that make SAP's innovations clear, engaging, and tangible.

02 · Projects

What I worked on

One of my main tasks was creating a consistent visual identity for the team's communication. This included designing a PowerPoint template for customer presentations and internal meetings, as well as a matching email banner. A key project was the SAP Experience Center Tokyo slide deck, designed for two audiences: an immersive customer-facing version inspired by Tokyo's neon aesthetics and Japanese cultural elements, and a clean, information-focused version for internal employees.

Beyond creating materials, I also had the opportunity to participate in real customer meetings, experiencing first-hand how Presales conversations work, how customers respond to visual storytelling, and how the materials I created are used in practice.

One of my key projects was designing the Autonomous Enterprise presentation for SAP's Immersive Experience Room in Tokyo. I developed visual concepts tailored to the room's 360° LED environment, combining bold typography, ambient gradients and dynamic layouts. The presentation covered a range of topics, from Joule to external data integration, bringing SAP's vision of the Autonomous Enterprise to life through an immersive visual experience.

The presentation in action during a live customer tour at SAP's Immersive Experience Room in Tokyo. Projected across floor-to-ceiling LED walls, the visuals created an immersive environment for introducing customers to SAP's vision of the Autonomous Enterprise.
