Flamigo Game Night

01 · Briefing & Core Idea

Background & Vision

In the Media Project: Basics module, the task was to develop a company from idea to business plan. The result: Flamigo e.U., a games start-up that brings generations together through creative mini-games — no matter the distance.

The core idea: True connection knows no distance. Flamigo turns shared game nights into moments of togetherness and closeness — playfully, intuitively, and with heart.

Project Goals

  • Develop a credible start-up with a clear vision & positioning
  • Design a playful, family-friendly brand (logo, color palette, flyer)
  • Create a complete business plan across ten chapters
  • Convey the values of closeness, togetherness & intergenerational play
accessible playful interactive family-oriented

Independent design and creation of the depicted gatefold flyer, from layout through visual language to copy.

03 · Flyer

The Brand Made Tangible

As the central print medium, a gatefold flyer was created that tells the story of vision, benefits, team, and game selection, consistently using the Flamigo visual world of bubbles, waves, and world map.

04 · The Product

One App, Three Experience Layers

01

Online Mini-Games

Play & Connect
  • Diverse mini-games for shared game nights
  • Daily challenges & point system for family and friends
  • New games & content every month
  • Intuitive to use, even for toddlers & older users
Freemium model · Free basic, premium optional
02

Board Game Bridge

Digital meets analog
  • Recommendations & sales of curated board games
  • Partnerships with sustainable, regional manufacturers
  • Own board game versions of popular app games planned
  • Seamless transition from the app to the living room table
Hybrid approach · digitally & physically connected
03

Family Features

Closeness in everyday life
  • Custom backgrounds, filters, stickers & avatars
  • Shared real-time shopping list & family calendar
  • Shared savings book & task distribution
  • Exclusive premium content for personalized experiences
Premium subscription · personalized family world

The interplay of mini-games, the bridge to board games, and practical family features makes Flamigo more than just a games app: a platform emerges that translates initial playful contact into real, lasting connection while making family life a little easier along the way.

05 · Corporate Design

A Brand with Heart & Wings

Playful, warm & distinctive

The Flamigo bird combines two silhouettes into one figure — a symbol of closeness and togetherness. The word mark takes the name in vibrant magenta, while „go“ in bold blue stands for movement and the start of shared play.

Round, soft shapes, floating bubbles, and a delicate world map in the background tell the core message: connection across any distance.

Magenta Pink Blue Lilac Rosé
06 · Conclusion

What I take away from this project.

Flamigo was my first experience leading a team and building a brand from scratch. I learned how to turn a vision into a coherent identity — from logo and color palette through business plan to print media. Most importantly, it taught me how to balance creative direction with team coordination and strategic thinking.
