An AI-powered microlearning app with personalised 3-minute sessions, gamification mechanics, and an endearing otter mascot that makes daily learning a joyful habit — not a chore.

„Your daily dose of knowledge — in just 3 minutes."

01 · Briefing & Core Idea

Background & Brief

Knowledge that fits into 3 minutes.

In the Mobile Commerce & Development module, the task was to design a mobile app concept from scratch. The result is Lutra, an AI-powered microlearning companion.

Traditional e-learning demands long sessions and rigid schedules. Lutra flips this model with hyper-personalised 3-minute sessions — bite-sized, joyful, and habit-forming. An otter mascot guides every session, turning learning into a daily ritual you actually look forward to.

Project Goals

  • Design a mobile app concept solving a real learning-behaviour problem
  • Develop an AI-driven personalisation engine concept for content delivery
  • Create a cohesive brand identity with mascot, colors & typography
  • Integrate gamification to drive daily retention and long-term habit formation
AI-personalised bite-sized gamified habit-forming
02 · App Screens & Mockups

The Experience Made Visible

From the onboarding flow through the daily session card to the gamification dashboard, every screen of Lutra is designed to feel inviting, clear, and delightful. The otter mascot appears throughout, providing encouragement and celebrating progress.

The daily home screen

The home screen keeps everything within one tap: the otter companion greets you, your collected fish-points show your streak at a glance, and a single bold “Start Learning” button removes any friction before today's three-minute session.

Warm illustration, generous spacing and a calm periwinkle palette make the screen feel like a cosy room rather than a dashboard.

03 · The Product

One App, Three Experience Layers

01

AI-Powered Sessions

Learn & Adapt
  • Personalised 3-minute microlearning sessions each day
  • AI selects content based on goals, pace, and past performance
  • Spaced-repetition algorithm for maximum knowledge retention
  • Topics span business, science, languages, creativity & more
Freemium model · Core sessions free, deep dives premium
02

Gamification Engine

Play & Progress
  • Daily streaks, XP points, and level-up milestones
  • Achievement badges for topic mastery and consistency
  • Friendly leaderboards with friends and community
  • Otter companion reacts and grows alongside the learner
Engagement-first design · habit loops & emotional rewards
03

Personalisation Hub

Your Learning DNA
  • Goal-setting wizard on first launch to define learning intent
  • Weekly AI-generated learning reports and skill-gap insights
  • Custom learning paths for certificates and skill stacking
  • Offline mode for uninterrupted learning anywhere
Premium subscription · full path control & analytics

The combination of intelligent content curation, playful gamification, and deep personalisation makes Lutra more than a learning app: it becomes a daily companion that grows with you, keeps you motivated, and ensures every three minutes genuinely advances your knowledge and career.

04 · Brand & Visual Identity

A Brand Built on Warmth & Curiosity

Curious, warm & trustworthy

The Lutra otter mascot is the emotional heart of the brand. With its amber glasses and wide-eyed curiosity, the otter embodies the spirit of lifelong learning — approachable, enthusiastic, and never intimidating. Every microlearning session is guided by this companion, creating a sense of consistency and warmth.

The color system pairs a confident signature navy with a soft periwinkle and a warm lavender amber, bridging the trustworthiness of established education with the playfulness of a modern app.

05 · Fazit

What I take away from this project.

Lutra taught me how to develop a complete app concept from a real problem to a polished brand — and how much impact a strong visual identity and a well-thought-out mascot can have on the overall user experience. I learned to think in systems: how AI personalisation, gamification, and emotional design work together to shape a product that people actually want to use every day.

Most importantly, the project showed me that the best digital products are not just functional — they are felt. That insight now guides the way I approach every design decision.
