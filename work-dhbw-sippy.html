Sippy Water Tracker

A native macOS menu-bar app that lives quietly in your menu bar and gently nudges you to drink water throughout the day — with hourly reminders, a friendly otter mascot, and a satisfying daily dashboard.

Stay hydrated, stay happy.

01 · The Idea

Why Sippy?

Drinking enough water every day sounds simple — but it's surprisingly easy to forget when you're deep in focus mode. Most hydration apps are either too complex, too loud, or feel like chores. Sippy takes a different approach: a lightweight menu-bar app that never interrupts your workflow, just gently reminds you to take a sip.

The idea was to build a truly native macOS experience — no Electron, no web wrappers — that feels at home on the desktop. Every hourly reminder, every tap of the menu-bar icon reveals a clean popover with your progress for the day. And accompanying you throughout is a little otter mascot whose mood changes depending on how well-hydrated you've kept yourself: sleepy when you've forgotten, cheerful when you're on track, and absolutely delighted when you hit your goal.

02 · Features

What Sippy Can Do

Hourly Reminders

Context-aware notifications fire quietly at the top of each hour, keeping you on track without derailing your focus. Snooze or log a glass in one click — directly from the notification.

Today Dashboard

The popover shows an animated water glass filling up as the day progresses, a clean progress bar toward your daily goal, and quick-add buttons for 50 ml to 500 ml — one tap and you're back to work.

Month Overview

A calendar grid gives you a bird's-eye view of the whole month. Each day renders a small circular progress ring — green when you hit your goal, blue when you were close, and empty when you missed it entirely.

Otter Mascot

Five distinct moods, an idle breathing animation, and occasional blinks make the otter feel alive. Entirely vector-based — drawn with SwiftUI Shape and Canvas — no external assets required.

04 · Under the Hood

Technical Details

Sippy is written entirely in Swift 6 with SwiftUI, targeting macOS 14+. The persistent menu-bar presence is powered by MenuBarExtra — Apple's modern, declarative API for menu-bar apps introduced in macOS 13. All data stays on-device: intake entries and preferences are stored as JSON via UserDefaults, keeping the app lightweight and completely privacy-first with no network calls whatsoever.

The otter mascot is drawn entirely with SwiftUI's vector graphics APIs: Canvas, Path, and custom Shape conformances. Mood transitions animate via withAnimation and matchedGeometryEffect, while the idle breathing loop uses a repeating Animation.easeInOut. High-resolution export for App Store screenshots is handled by ImageRenderer.
