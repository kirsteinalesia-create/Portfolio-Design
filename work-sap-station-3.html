01 · Context

The Research LAB

My team, CX Cross, develops and maintains the Research LAB — an internal platform at SAP that supports user researchers in planning, conducting and documenting their studies. During this practical phase, I had the opportunity to write my second project paper (Project Paper 2) about this very tool: I analyzed the existing Research LAB, explored how Artificial Intelligence could enhance its workflows, and developed a concrete concept to integrate AI into the research process.

02 · Process

From research to prototype

I started by conducting expert interviews with researchers across SAP to understand how the Research LAB was being used, what worked well, and where the biggest pain points were. This gave me a clear picture of the status quo and helped me define the requirements for an AI-supported solution.

In parallel, I carried out a structured comparison of different AI research tools, evaluating them against criteria like usefulness, ease of integration and data privacy — a critical factor when dealing with user research data at SAP.

The Research LAB brings together all workspaces, an integrated Joule assistant for context-specific questions, and a dedicated chat for broader research inquiries — everything researchers need in one place.

Based on my findings, I designed an AI-supported research report template that automates parts of the documentation process and helps researchers structure their insights more efficiently. The template was integrated into a working prototype within the existing Research LAB, complete with a feedback loop involving the user research team to validate and refine the concept.
